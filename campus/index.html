Getting around

W A S D
Walk (arrow keys work too). Hold Shift to run.
Mouse
Click the view, then move the mouse to look. Esc gives the cursor back.
Space
Jump.
1–6
Jump to a viewpoint: the quad, the front, Building B, the gym, the stadium, the creek.
N R
Day or night, rain or dry.
H
Hide everything but the view (for screenshots).
F
Fly up for the aerial view, and back down. Up there: drag to turn, right-drag to slide, scroll to zoom, double-click the ground to land.
M
The map: click anywhere to jump there, or type a room number.
Room plates
Point at a room number on a wall and click (or press E) to open that room on Wilkipedia.
On a phone
Left thumb walks, right thumb looks. Double-tap the ground from the air to land.

Built from the official campus map and satellite measurements. The streets, houses and creek around the school come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Room interiors come building by building.
