Community rules

These keep Wilkipedia useful, and keep it online.

  1. No real tests, quizzes or answer keys. Describe the test style (“30 multiple choice + 2 free response, curved”). Never post the questions.
  2. About the class, not the person. Nothing insulting or personal about any teacher or student. Teacher sections are facts about how the class runs.
  3. Link, don’t upload, copyrighted material. Say where to find textbooks and teacher packets. Study guides you made yourself are welcome.
  4. Facts need a source. Grading weights and policies should come from the syllabus or the teacher. Opinions are fine, but they’re labelled as student experience.
  5. Say when. Everything shows the school year it applies to. Policies change, so hit “Report outdated” when something’s wrong.
  6. Don’t make things up. If you don’t know, leave it blank. An empty page is better than a wrong one.
  7. Use your real first name or a name people know you by. No impersonating anyone.

Reviewers remove anything that breaks these rules. Repeated problems mean losing the ability to post.

Wilkipedia is written by Wilcox students, for Wilcox students. It is an independent student project, not an official Wilcox High School or SCUSD site.
