How a bounty runs

  1. Claim it. Several people can claim the same bounty; reviewers merge the best parts. Claims expire after 14 days.
  2. Build it with the form. Required fields must be filled.
  3. Submit. It goes to the review queue, not straight onto the site.
  4. Review. A reviewer approves it or sends it back with a note saying what to fix.
  5. Credit. Approved work goes live with your name, and the points go on the leaderboard. When the job is fully done, an admin marks the bounty complete and it moves to the Ledger.

Rank is how urgent a bounty is: S critical · A high · B normal · C low · D whenever.

Effort and points: S (~30 min) = 10 · M (~2 hrs) = 30 · L (~5+ hrs) = 60 · anything outside a bounty = 5. After 3 approved submissions you become Trusted. If you signed in with your school account, your comments then post instantly.
