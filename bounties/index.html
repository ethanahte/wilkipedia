Bounty board

Wilkipedia is written by volunteers. Each bounty is one clear job with a finish line. Claim one, do it, submit it, and a reviewer publishes it with your name on it.

How a bounty runs

  1. Claim it. Several people can claim the same bounty; reviewers merge the best parts. Claims expire after 14 days.
  2. Build it with the form. Required fields must be filled.
  3. Submit. It goes to the review queue, not straight onto the site.
  4. Review. A reviewer approves it or sends it back with a note saying what to fix.
  5. Credit. Approved work goes live with your name, and the points go on the leaderboard.

Points: S (~30 min) = 10 · M (~2 hrs) = 30 · L (~5+ hrs) = 60 · anything outside a bounty = 5. After 3 approved submissions you become Trusted: your comments post instantly.

Have an idea for a bounty? Suggest it in a comment on the class page, or tell a reviewer.

Wilkipedia is written by Wilcox students, for Wilcox students. It is an independent student project, not an official Wilcox High School or SCUSD site.
