About Wilkipedia

A student-built guide to every class at Wilcox High School in Santa Clara.

Started in 2026 by Ethan Liu and Jonathan. Students write everything through bounties, and reviewers check it before it’s published.

Where the facts come from. Course names, grade levels, prerequisites and descriptions come from the SCUSD High School Course Catalog 2025–2026. Teacher lists come from the Wilcox staff directory. Everything else is written by students.

Not official. Wilkipedia is an independent student project, not a Wilcox High School or Santa Clara Unified site. Always confirm policies and deadlines with your teacher or counselor.

Something wrong? Every section has a “Report outdated” button. To reach the team, comment on any class page or tell a reviewer.

Wilkipedia is written by Wilcox students, for Wilcox students. It is an independent student project, not an official Wilcox High School or SCUSD site.
