Catalog description

In this class, students will learn to create and produce professional-grade videos that advance both creative and technical skills. Collaboration is required for every project, both inside and outside of school. Students will learn advanced levels of pre-production, production, and post-production methodology and practices, and will take on multiple roles including director, copywriter, storyboard artist, camera person, sound recordist, lighting technician, on-camera talent, production assistant, computer editor, and graphic artist. *Video Production classes count towards Practical or Fine Arts credits
