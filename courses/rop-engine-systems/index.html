Catalog description

The second semester in Engine Systems, students will develop knowledge, skills, and self-confidence in identifying and describing the mechanical systems of a modern automotive engine. Students will be able to perform basic maintenance and repair of automotive engine systems that include a lubrication system, cooling system, fuel system, engine management systems, and gaskets and seals. S/he will be able to identify and describe the function of components for each system. During the course, the student will work collaboratively while completing classwork and lab activities. The students will practice shop safety, use tools properly, and maintain a clean, environmentally favorable work area.

From the SCUSD High School Course Catalog 2025–2026, page 97.
