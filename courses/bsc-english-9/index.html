Catalog description

Students will work on basic writing and reading skills, one-on-one and in groups. Correct grammar and punctuation will be focused on as well as word analysis and fluency. The students read and analyze English literature, novels, and short stories. Vocabulary and comprehension will be improved through spelling, reading assignments, and group discussions. Levels and materials will vary according to each individual’s goals. It follows the California State Standards with modified instruction and meets the English graduation requirements. Students with active IEPs will receive appropriate modifications and accommodations to access the curriculum described above.

From the SCUSD High School Course Catalog 2025–2026, page 51.
