Catalog description

Through this course, students will practice the skills required to produce plays and musicals. Whether an actor or technician, students can gain the experience of going from audition to performance in this class. Students must audition for specific roles and.or interview for technical positions, then accept these positions to remain in class. Students are required to rehearse or work many more hours beyond class time, as well as attend all performances. Students will receive elective credit.
